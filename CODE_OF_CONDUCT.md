# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free collaboration environment. Be considerate, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, accept constructive feedback, and prioritize the community's safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information, deliberate disruption, and retaliation. Report concerns privately to `support@teampsmpv.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, patterns of behavior, and the safety of affected people. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
